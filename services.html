Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, straightforward answers about how we build and deliver digital projects.

Why Vanilla HTML, CSS & JavaScript?

Vanilla code eliminates unnecessary dependencies, security vulnerabilities, and build tool breakage. It results in pages that load instantly, require minimal hosting costs, and remain maintainable for years without complex updates.

How long does a website project take?

Most standard business websites and landing pages are completed within 7 to 14 business days, depending on scope, content preparation, and review speed.

Will my website work properly on mobile phones?

Yes, 100%. Every project is built mobile-first and tested thoroughly across iPhone, Android, tablets, and desktop resolutions.

How do inquiries reach my business?

We integrate direct WhatsApp routing, click-to-call buttons, and mailto email triggers. We also offer integration with third-party form services (such as Formspree or EmailJS) for direct inbox delivery.
